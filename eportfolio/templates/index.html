Analysing Mental Health in Student Ecosystem

An interactive Tableau analysis of 1,000 student records: how sleep, study habits, attendance, stress and depression relate to mental health scores and risk levels.

What this project found

  • No single factor predicts risk: stress, depression and high-risk share are close across every gender.
  • Study hours and sleep show almost no relationship with academic score or stress.
  • Risk is spread evenly, so broad, inclusive support fits better than targeting one group.
